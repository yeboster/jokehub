# edit-joke

## Overview
Route group for editing a joke. This directory has no source files of its own.

## Subdirectories & Boundaries
- [jokeId]: [./[jokeId]/EGM.md](<./[jokeId]/EGM.md>) - `/edit-joke/[jokeId]`. An owner-only client form (zod with `react-hook-form`) for updating `text`, `category`, `source`, `funnyRate` (0 to 5) and `used`, or for deleting the joke.
